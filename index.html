STUMPS & SIXES

2D cricket batting · real ball physics · time your shot

PLAYERS

Each player keeps their own scores. Pick who is batting.
Switching player during a match starts a fresh innings (the current one is saved for the player who was batting).

HOW TO PLAY

THE IDEA

A bowler runs in and bowls a red ball every 5 seconds. You are the batsman: press swing so the bat meets the ball at exactly the right instant. The better your timing, the better the shot.

SCORING BY TIMING

PERFECTwithin ±20 ms of the sweet spot — best power and placement
Goodwithin ±50 ms
Averagewithin ±100 ms
Poor / Edgemistimed or off the edge
Missno contact
After each shot you see the label, your timing in ms, the ball’s exit speed and the runs.

RUNS & BOUNDARIES

  • FOUR: the ball bounces, then crosses the rope.
  • SIX: the ball crosses the rope without touching the ground first.
  • This applies to any shot — even an edge that crosses the rope.
  • A ball that stops short scores 0–3 depending on how well you hit it.

HOW YOU GET OUT

Only one way: BOWLED — you miss the ball and it hits the stumps. Edges, mishits and lofted shots are never out.

DELIVERIES & LEVELS

Expect fast balls, slower balls, yorkers, bouncers, swing (in/out) and spin (off-break, leg-break, googly, doosra). Five levels — Club Nets, County Cup, Test Match, Day-Night Final and Storm Chasers — each faster than the last. Reach each level’s run target to move up.
